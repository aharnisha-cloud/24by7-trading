## Criteria (what counts as a setup)

Define your rules here so the AI scans consistently. Examples:

- Trend: price above/below the 50-period moving average
- Trigger: breakout of the last 20 candles' high/low, or pullback to support
- Risk: stop-loss always defined before entry; minimum 2:1 reward:risk
- Max open paper positions: 3
- Risk per trade: 1% of paper account ($10,000 starting paper balance)
